# Principal guide (and the school owner)

English only; Telugu material is deferred ([ADR-0036](../adr/ADR-0036-english-first-telugu-hidden.md)).
All examples are synthetic. Start with the [common section](README.md#things-every-staff-member-should-know)
on signing in and "Confirm it's you".

You are the school's **second person**: most changes that matter wait for you. Principals and owners
must use two-step verification (MFA), and most approvals ask you to **Confirm it's you**.

The **owner** approves corrections and certificates (sections 1 and 2), approves notices (section 5,
but does not send them), decides privacy requests and manages breaches (sections 9 and 10) and runs
school admin (section 11). The owner does not do office work: no imports, waivers, fee approvals,
fee messages, attendance or marks.

## Your day at a glance

**Home** shows the work waiting for you: **Blocking problems**, **Changes waiting for approval**,
**Fee requests for you**, **Portal readiness** and **Today's day book**. Start every day there.

## 1. Approve correction requests

1. Open **Correction requests** (or select **Open change requests** on **Home**).
2. Open a request. Check **The change** against the **Evidence** document.
3. Select **Approve** (optionally add a note) or **Reject** with a reason of at least 10 characters,
   for example "The birth certificate shows a different date".
4. **Confirm it's you** when asked.

Approving changes the student's record at once; the old value stays in the history. You cannot
approve a request you made yourself ("Someone else must decide this").

## 2. Approve certificates

1. Open **Certificates**. Requests waiting for you show **Waiting for your decision**.
2. Open one and read **What the certificate prints**.
3. Select **Approve and issue** (it gets the next serial number and goes into the register) or
   **Reject** with a reason.
4. Print with **Open print view**.

On a TC you may see **Fees outstanding (for your information)**. It is a note for you only: it is
never printed on the TC and never stops it.

**Registers** shows the TC register (counterfoil), the certificate issue register and the admission
and withdrawal register for printing. You may be asked to confirm it's you.

## 3. Checks, readiness and portal windows

- **Check before submitting**: blockers must be cleared before a portal submission. Only you, the
  owner or an office admin can **Accept as it is** (waive) a problem; write why it is right as it is.
- **Board readiness**: how many students are ready for each board and portal; print parent slips by
  section.
- **Portal calendar**: when a department circular gives the dates, select **Confirm the dates** and
  choose **Who owns the work**. The owner of the work gets a task and reminders 30, 14, 7 and 1 days
  before the window closes.

Details of each step are in the [office guide, section 2](office.md#2-checks-readiness-and-the-portal-calendar).

## 4. Fees: approvals and the day book

1. Open **Fee approvals**. You see concessions, receipt cancellations and refunds that someone else
   asked for.
2. Read the reason. Select **Approve** or **Reject**, and confirm it's you.
   - An approved concession is posted to the student's account at once.
   - An approved refund writes a refund voucher today.
3. **Daily collection** shows the day's receipts, **Cash to hand over** and
   **Cheques to hand over**. The accountant usually selects **Close day**; you can too.
4. **Fee dues**: select students and **Send reminder** or **Send payment link** (needs WhatsApp and
   online payments switched on). You are the only standard role that can send these.

## 5. Parent notices and WhatsApp

1. Open **Parent notices**. Drafts made by the office wait for you.
2. Open a draft, check every date and word, then select **Approve notice**.
3. Send it with **Send on WhatsApp**: choose the sections, **Check who will get it**, then
   **Send to … parents**. Or **Print (A4)**.
4. If a notice was wrong, select **Withdraw notice**: parent links stop working and messages not yet
   sent are cancelled.

**WhatsApp** (in **School admin**) holds the school's WhatsApp connection, quiet hours and automatic
messages. Changing it asks you to confirm it's you.

**Messages and estimated Meta cost** (on the same screen) shows how many WhatsApp messages went out
each month and roughly what Meta will charge, including GST. The school pays Meta directly; Meta's
invoice has the final amount. Since 1 October 2026 Meta charges every utility message, also when
the parent wrote first, and replies above 1,000 a month. Choose a month under **Show details for**
to see it by purpose (notices, absence alerts, fee messages). If the card says **Meta may have
changed its rates**, the estimate uses older rates until SchoolOS updates them.

If the screen says **Move WhatsApp billing to Indian rupees by 31/12/2026**, the school's Meta account
is billed in another currency. Follow the steps shown (WhatsApp Manager → payment settings → change
the currency to INR, then pay anything still due). From 1 January 2027 Meta stops delivering messages
from accounts not billed in rupees.

## 6. Circulars and tasks

Open **Circulars**, review the **Suggested deadlines** of each uploaded circular (**Confirm as task**
or **Dismiss**), then **Mark reviewed**. **Tasks** shows every open task in the school; you can add,
edit and reassign them. Details: [office guide, section 7](office.md#7-circulars-and-tasks).

## 7. Attendance, marks and students to follow up

- **Attendance** and **Marks** work as in the [class teacher guide](class-teacher.md), for every
  section.
- **Students to follow up** lists early-warning flags (attendance, marks). Use it for teaching and
  child safety only, never for anything else. You can change **How flags are raised** and assign a
  flag to a teacher with **Give to someone else**.

## 8. Ask the school

Ask in plain English and check the **Sources** under every answer. "Not found in the school records
you can access" means SchoolOS did not find it in what you may see; it does not guess. Save good
answers with **Save as verified answer**. Details: [office guide, section 9](office.md#9-ask-the-school).

## 9. Privacy requests

1. Open **Requests and complaints**. Each request shows its **Due** date; overdue ones are listed
   separately.
2. Open a request. Check **How we checked who asked** before deciding.
3. Depending on what was asked:
   - access: **Build the report**, check it, then **Release the report** (ready to download for 24
     hours; hand it to the parent yourself);
   - correction: use correction requests as usual;
   - erasure: follow the erasure plan the page shows (records the law requires the school to keep are
     kept).
4. When the person has their answer, select **Close the request** (optionally **Close with this
   answer**) and **Print answer**. A closed request cannot be opened again.
5. If the person withdraws, select **Mark as withdrawn**.

Decisions ask you to confirm it's you. Someone else must close a request you logged yourself.

**Notices** (under **Privacy**) holds the privacy notice templates. After the school gives one out,
select **Record that we issued it**; tick **Also make the PDF of this notice** for an A4 copy to keep.

## 10. Breaches (principal and owner)

A breach is any loss, leak or wrong access of personal data (for example a lost pen drive with a fee
list, or a message sent to the wrong parent group).

1. Open **Breach register** and select **Record a breach**. Write what happened. Never copy the
   leaked data into the record.
2. To count the people affected, select **Count from the school's records**, tick sections or find
   students (and staff), select **Count**, then **Use these counts**. Only the numbers are kept, never
   the list.
3. The record shows the duties that follow and the time left on each. When a duty is done, select
   **Mark done** and say when and how. This cannot be changed later.
4. Under **Drafts**, check each draft letter. Use **Print** or **Open the print page** for an A4 copy.
   **Draft the family notice** makes a parent notice draft that you approve and send like any notice.
   SchoolOS never sends a breach notice by itself.
5. When everything is done, select **Close the breach**. A closed breach cannot be changed.

If SchoolOS itself has an incident that affects your school, it appears in the register with the
facts SchoolOS gives you.

## 11. School admin

- **Users and roles**: **Invite user**, choose the role and, for teachers, the classes and sections
  they may see. Changes are recorded in the audit log.
- **Support access**: approve or deny a SchoolOS support request; you can revoke it at any time.
- **Audit log**: every important action, with who did it and when.
- **Plan and billing**: your plan, AI answers used this month and invoices.

## 12. Safety checks and school policies

- **Safety checks** (**Compliance > Safety checks**): after a fire drill, extinguisher refill,
  electrical, water or CCTV check, click **Log a check** and type the date, who led it, for drills
  how many people took part and how many minutes it took, and what was found. Type counts and
  short notes only: never a student's name, and never upload photos that show children. The list
  shows what is overdue or never logged; SchoolOS reminds the office before the next one is due.
- **Policies** (**Compliance > Policies**): upload the policy as a PDF in Documents (purpose
  policy), then **Add a policy**, choose who must read it (for example teachers and class
  teachers) and the next review date. Open a policy to see who has and has not read it, and
  **Remind staff who have not read it**. After the yearly review, click **Mark reviewed**.
- **Policies to read**: every staff member sees the policies asked of them, opens each one and
  clicks **I have read this version**. A new version of the document needs a new confirmation.

## If something goes wrong

- "Not done: we still need to confirm it's you": the step-up window was closed. Try again and choose
  **Sign in again**.
- "This request was already decided" or "This request has expired": reload the list; ask the office
  to send a new request if needed.
- "The value changed after this request was made": the record changed since; reject it and ask for a
  new request.
- A WhatsApp message went to the wrong people: open **Messages** and select **Stop link** on it, then
  record a breach if personal data was shared.
