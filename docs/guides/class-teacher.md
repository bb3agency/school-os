# Class teacher guide (and teacher)

English only; Telugu material is deferred ([ADR-0036](../adr/ADR-0036-english-first-telugu-hidden.md)).
All examples are synthetic. Start with the [common section](README.md#things-every-staff-member-should-know)
on signing in.

You see **only the sections you teach**. If a section is missing, ask the office admin to add it to
your account on **Users and roles**.

**Class teacher** can: take attendance, enter marks, see and act on students to follow up, record a
parent's WhatsApp decision, see the problems and readiness of their sections, read tasks and
documents, and ask the school.

**Teacher** (a subject teacher without a class) can see the students of their sections, read tasks
and documents, and ask the school. Sections 1-4 below do not apply to the teacher role.

**Home** shows **Your work today**: **Take attendance**, **Enter marks** and **Ask the school**.

## 1. Attendance

**Every morning**

1. Open **Attendance** (or **Take attendance** on **Home**).
2. Choose your **Section**. **Show** is **One day**; the **Date** is today.
3. Select **Mark everyone present**, then change the few who are **Absent**, **Late** or on
   **Leave**.
4. Select **Save attendance**. The page says how many entries were saved.

If you made a mistake before saving, select **Undo changes**. After saving, change the mark and save
again.

If the school has switched on absence alerts on WhatsApp, the parents of children marked absent get
one message later in the day. Mark attendance on time and carefully.

**The month register**

1. Choose **Month register** under **Show** and pick the **Month**.
2. Select **Print the register** for the A4 copy. P is present, A absent, L late, LV leave.

**Old months from paper.** **Add a month from the register sheet** reads a sheet (XLSX or CSV) with
admission number, name and one column per day. You see what was read and any problems; nothing is
saved until you confirm.

## 2. Marks

The exam coordinator creates the exams. You enter marks for your sections.

1. Open **Marks** (or **Enter marks** on **Home**).
2. Choose the **Section** and the **Exam**.
3. In **Enter marks for one subject**, choose the **Subject** and type the **Maximum marks**.
4. Type each student's marks. Type **AB** for absent. Leave a box empty to skip a student. Press
   **Enter** or the down arrow to move to the next student.
5. Select **Save marks**.

To enter a whole sheet at once, use **Add marks from a sheet** (row 1: admission or roll number and
one column per subject; row 2: maximum marks; then one row per student).

## 3. Students to follow up

SchoolOS raises a **follow-up flag** from fixed rules (for example three school days absent in a row,
falling marks or repeated behaviour notes). Nothing here is decided by AI. Use it only for the
child's learning and safety; never share it outside the school.

1. Open **Students to follow up**. Overdue flags are marked **Overdue**.
2. Open a flag and read why it was raised and **What has been done**.
3. After you act (for example you called the parent), select **Log an action**, choose
   **What did you do?**, the date and an optional note, then **Save action**.
4. When the matter is settled, select **Close the flag**, choose the reason and **Close flag**. If someone else should handle it, the principal
   can give the flag to them.

To record behaviour, open the student and select **Add a behaviour note** (at most 500 characters;
facts only, no Aadhaar numbers). If you see a concern no rule has caught, select
**Raise a flag** on the student's page.

Only you (the class teacher of that section) and the principal see these notes and flags.

## 4. WhatsApp agreement from parents

Parents get WhatsApp messages from the school only if they agreed.

1. Open the student and find **WhatsApp messages**.
2. Select **Record a decision**. Choose **Messages about** (for example school notices), the
   **Decision** and the **Date of the decision**. For a signed form, the office uploads the form first
   and gives you its document ID.
3. Select **Save**. Every decision is kept with your name; nothing is overwritten.

If a line says "The number changed after this: ask again", ask the parent again and record the new
decision.

## 5. Problems in your section's records

**Check before submitting** and **Board readiness** show the problems and readiness of your sections.
You cannot fix records yourself: print the list (**Print**) or the parent slips and give them to the
office, or ask parents to bring the right documents.

## 6. Tasks and circulars

**Tasks** shows the school's open tasks with their due dates. If a task is yours, do it and tell the
office; the office or principal marks it done.

## 7. Ask the school

1. Open **Ask the school** (or **Ask the school** on **Home**).
2. Type a question, for example "Which students in Class VII-B were absent more than five days in
   October?" and select **Ask**.
3. Check the **Sources** under the answer. You only get answers from your own sections and the
   documents you may see.
4. "Not found in the school records you can access" means SchoolOS did not find it in what you may
   see. It does not guess.
5. Tell us if the answer helped with **Was this answer helpful?**.

## If something goes wrong

- "You can't see attendance" or "You can't see marks": your account has no class role or no section.
  Ask the office admin.
- "Type a number from 0 to …, or AB": a mark is above the maximum or not a number.
- "No students in this section": students are not placed in the section yet; ask the office.
- You marked a child absent by mistake and the alert may go out: correct the mark and save at once,
  then tell the office so they can call the parent if the message was already sent.

## Cheat sheet (print this page)

**Class teacher** · SchoolOS · synthetic examples only

| I want to… | Go to | Press |
|---|---|---|
| Take today's attendance | **Attendance** → my section | **Mark everyone present** → change absentees → **Save attendance** |
| Print the month register | **Attendance** → **Month register** | **Print the register** |
| Enter marks | **Marks** → section + exam | subject, **Maximum marks** → type (AB = absent) → **Save marks** |
| Import marks | **Marks** | **Add marks from a sheet** → confirm |
| Act on a flag | **Students to follow up** → flag | **Log an action** → **Save action** / **Close the flag** |
| Write a behaviour note | student page | **Add a behaviour note** → **Save note** |
| Record a parent's WhatsApp yes/no | student page → **WhatsApp messages** | **Record a decision** → **Save** |
| See my section's problems | **Check before submitting** | **Print** for the office |
| Ask a question | **Ask the school** | **Ask**, check **Sources** |

**Remember:** attendance before the first period ends · AB for absent in marks · flags are for the
child's learning and safety only · never type an Aadhaar number · leaving the computer →
**Lock now**.
