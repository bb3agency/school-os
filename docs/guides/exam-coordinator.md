# Exam coordinator guide

English only; Telugu material is deferred ([ADR-0036](../adr/ADR-0036-english-first-telugu-hidden.md)).
All examples are synthetic. Start with the [common section](README.md#things-every-staff-member-should-know)
on signing in and "Confirm it's you".

You own two things: **exams and marks**, and getting student records **ready for board and portal
submissions** (board registration, UDISE+ and similar) before each window closes.

## What your role can do

- **Marks**: create exams, enter and import marks for every section, see results.
- **Check before submitting**, **Board readiness**, **Portal calendar**: run checks, resolve problems,
  set portal dates and owners.
- **Correction requests**: ask for corrections (the principal approves).
- **Imports**: upload, check and add spreadsheets (for example a board registration list).
- **Exports**: pre-check sheets for boards and portals (confirm it's you).
- **APAAR consent** and **Consents**: see who has agreed.
- **Ask the school**, **Tasks**, **Documents**.

You cannot accept (waive) a problem as it is: ask the principal or office admin.

## 1. Exams and marks

**Create an exam**

1. Open **Marks**. Choose a **Section**.
2. Under **New exam**, type the **Exam name** (for example "Unit test 2") and the **Date**, then
   **Create exam**.

**Enter marks for one subject**

1. Choose the **Section** and the **Exam**.
2. In **Enter marks for one subject**, choose the **Subject** and type the **Maximum marks**.
3. Type each student's marks. Type **AB** for absent. Leave a box empty to skip a student. Press
   **Enter** or the down arrow to go to the next student.
4. Select **Save marks**.

**Add marks from a sheet**

1. Make a sheet: row 1 has the admission or roll number, then one column per subject; row 2 has the
   maximum marks; then one row per student (AB for absent).
2. Use **Add marks from a sheet** on **Marks**, choose the file, check the summary
   ("Read … marks for … students") and confirm. Nothing is saved until you confirm.

Class teachers enter marks for their own sections the same way. You see all sections.

### If something goes wrong

- "Type a number from 0 to …, or AB": a mark is above the maximum or not a number.
- "No students in this section": students are not placed in the section yet; ask the office.

## 2. Getting ready for a portal window

Work through these in order, starting at least a month before the window.

1. **Portal calendar**: when the circular gives the dates, select **Confirm the dates**, type the
   opening and closing dates, and set **Who owns the work** (often you). You get a task and reminders
   30, 14, 7 and 1 days before it closes. Dates marked "Expected dates are not official" are last
   year's pattern only.
2. **Check before submitting**: select **Start check**. Filter to the blockers for the board or
   portal. Open each problem and fix it (section 3).
3. **Board readiness**: choose the board or portal. Select **Start the check**. Open each section to
   see who is not ready and why. Use **Print slips for this section** to send parent slips home to
   correct details.
4. **Exports**: when readiness is high, open **Exports**, choose **Pre-check before you submit**
   (**New pre-check**), choose the **Format** (the board or portal), then **Make the pre-check**.
   Confirm it's you, then download the file. Its "ready to enter" sheet lists the fields in the
   portal's order, so you can copy them in.

Every export is recorded in the audit log. Keep downloaded files on the office computer only, and
delete them after the submission.

## 3. Fixing a problem

On a problem's page you see **What was found**, **Values from each source** and **How to fix it**.

- The record is wrong in an identity field (name, date of birth, parent name): select
  **Request a correction**, type the **Correct value** exactly as on the evidence, attach the
  evidence with **Upload a file now** and **Send for approval**. The principal approves.
- You corrected it another way: select **Resolve** and write **What was done**. Resolving a blocker
  asks you to confirm it's you.
- The values are right as they are: ask the principal or office admin to **Accept as it is**.

SchoolOS never changes a record by itself. The next check looks again; a problem that is still there
comes back.

## 4. APAAR consent

**APAAR consent** shows, for each student, whether the parent's consent for the APAAR ID is recorded.
APAAR IDs print on certificates only when verified and consent is given. Ask the office to record
consents; you can see them.

## 5. Ask the school

Examples: "Which students in Class X have no board registration number?" or "When does the board
registration window close?". Check the **Sources** under each answer. "Not found in the school
records you can access" means there is no matching record you may see; SchoolOS does not guess.

## If something goes wrong

- "Format not yet confirmed" on readiness: the board's format has not been confirmed by a school yet.
  Compare the pre-check with the portal before you rely on it.
- The export asks you to confirm it's you and the window closes: allow pop-ups, or choose
  **Continue in this tab**.
- A correction is stuck: open **Correction requests** to see whether it was approved, rejected (with
  the reason) or has expired.
