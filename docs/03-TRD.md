## 1. System summary

A multi-tenant web application (Next.js BFF + FastAPI API + Celery workers) on AWS ap-south-1 with PostgreSQL (pgvector) as the system of record and S3 for files. An LLM gateway calls Google Gemini on Vertex AI in asia-south1 (ADR-0033; Anthropic Claude as a config-selectable fallback); an embeddings provider interface serves retrieval. It is offered as a managed SaaS in two tiers from one codebase: the **shared tier** (pooled platform) and the **dedicated tier** (one isolated host per school). A control plane (platform admin panel, billing, fleet) runs only in the shared deployment (ADR-0015, ADR-0017). See 04-Architecture §16 and 16-Platform admin panel.

Details the implementation decided:
- **Gate (FR-FEE-001).** Fees are on when the per-school flag `fees.enabled` is on and the school setting `fees_enabled` is true (`PATCH /tenant`, `tenant.settings.manage`ᴿ, audited with its value). `fees` is listed under `alongside` in `operating_modes.yaml` and in `modules_hidden` whenever the setting is off; the setting is how the owner turns fees on despite the ERP. Off: every `/fees` route answers 404 `module_hidden`.
- **Plans (FR-FEE-003/004/005).** A plan is edited only as a draft; any active plan is frozen (trigger `fee_plans_frozen`), not only one with posted charges. Instalment amounts must add up per head (422 `instalments_do_not_add_up`) and due dates must fall inside the academic year (422 `due_outside_year`). `POST /fees/plans/{id}/new-version` copies the active plan as a draft; activating it supersedes the previous version; `GET /fees/plans/{id}/differences` and `POST /fees/plans/{id}/adjustments` turn the per-student differences into `plan_change` adjustment requests (unique per account, instalment, head and plan) and move the accounts to the new version. Applying a plan is **one transaction, not a background job** (`dry_run` gives the summary first; ≤ 2,000 students per call; `heavy_jobs` budget); it is idempotent through the unique charge index. A student whose active enrolment started after an instalment's due date gets only later instalments unless `include_past_instalments`.
- **Dues.** A line is (instalment, head); the late fee of an instalment is its own line (no head). Overdue amounts and the counter's "collectable" total count only collectable heads (GST-exempt; taxable heads are planned and charged but never collected, FR-FEE-023). Credits (a line below zero after a concession) are allowed; receipts never exceed the positive collectable dues (no advances).
- **Receipts (FR-FEE-010/011/022).** Modes at the counter: `cash`, `cheque` (6-digit number, bank ≤ 80 characters, cheque date), `bank_transfer` (reference of 12-22 letters or digits, typed field like the UTR), `upi_qr` (UTR). The number is allocated last in the transaction (`RCT/2026-27/000123`, `config.yaml`); a deferred constraint trigger checks at commit that allocations and payment entries equal the amount. The frozen content (school, number, dates, student name, admission number and class, lines, amount and words, mode, collector, GST note, paper size, template) is hashed with SHA-256 over canonical JSON. The PDF is a `fee_receipt` document (never indexed for Ask, never listed to the model). A cancelled receipt's PDF download answers 409 `receipt_cancelled`; its print view is marked CANCELLED.
- **Requests (FR-FEE-007/008/012/013).** Concessions: `percent` of each target line's charge (rounded half up to the paisa) or an `amount` spread over the targets in order, never more than the charge less earlier concessions (409 `nothing_to_concede`). Adjustments are requested with `fee.manage` and approved with `fee.concession.approve`ᴿ; a late-fee waiver has no head and a negative amount. A receipt with a refund (asked for or paid) is not cancelled (409 `receipt_has_refunds`). Cash, cheque and bank-transfer refunds are written as processed vouchers (`RFD/2026-27/0001`) on approval, spread over the receipt's lines newest instalment first; online refunds (`original_method`) stay with R2-2D. Every request can be withdrawn by its maker while pending.
- **Late fees (FR-FEE-009).** Keys are `flat:<instalment>` and `day:<instalment>:<YYYYMMDD>`, unique per account; a new plan version does not post a flat late fee again. Per-day late fees catch up missed nights (up to `late_fees.max_catch_up_days`) and the last one is trimmed to the cap. Only collectable lines count as "still due".
- **Day close (FR-FEE-019).** `POST /fees/day-closes` with `{"date"}` (today or up to 31 days back); receipts, cancellations and refund approvals take a shared advisory lock on (school, date) and closing takes it exclusive, so nothing is dated a day after its close commits.
- **Reports (FR-FEE-018/020).** The CSVs carry a UTF-8 BOM, cells are formula-safe (`app.core.spreadsheet.safe_cell`), and both downloads are audited as `fee.report.downloaded`.

**As built (R2-2E, 2026-10-18; `app/fees/upi.py`, `statements.py`, `statements_parse.py`, `upi_config.py` + `upi.yaml`, `upi_qr.py`, `api_upi.py`, `upi_tasks.py`; migration `0060_fees_upi`; tests `apps/api/tests/fees/test_upi_*.py`).** FR-FEE-014..017 are built. Choices the implementation made:
- **UPI settings and QR (FR-FEE-015).** `fin.upi_settings` holds the payee name (2-60 characters), the UPI ID (shape only, `^[A-Za-z0-9][A-Za-z0-9._-]{1,63}@[A-Za-z][A-Za-z0-9]{1,63}$`, never checked with a bank) and `confirm_within_days` (1-30, default 7). The static QR is **generated** (segno, inline SVG) from `upi://pay?pa=<vpa>&pn=<payee>&cu=INR` with no amount, instead of storing an uploaded image: it is what a bank's static QR encodes, needs no image upload, scan or storage, and cannot carry anything else. `GET /fees/upi-settings/qr.svg` serves it with `Cache-Control: private, no-store`, `default-src 'none'` and `nosniff`. Audit `fee.upi.settings_updated` carries whether the payee changed, the window and the version, never the UPI ID.
- **UTR at the counter (FR-FEE-014).** `POST /fees/upi/utr-check` (`fee.collect`, UTR in the body): `invalid` / `free` / `in_use` (receipt id and number) and `seen_in_bank` (a credit with this UTR is imported; no amount at the counter). Issuing is unchanged (R2-1B).
- **Statement import (FR-FEE-016).** `POST /fees/bank-statements` answers **201 synchronously** (a statement is at most 2 MB and 5,000 transactions): the caller's own `import_file` (ACL: the uploader's membership) is read and deleted in its own committed transaction (`bank_statement_read`, audit `fee.statement.read`), so the file never lingers whatever the parse finds; the same file (SHA-256) is refused (409 `statement_already_imported`). Presets (`upi.yaml`, `verified: false` until a pilot school confirms them, D3): generic, SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Kotak (amount with Dr/Cr); the heading row is found in the first 40 rows; detection tries the bank presets, then the generic one. The whole file is refused (422 with row numbers) for a formula cell, an Aadhaar number written as printed (`1234 5678 9012`, Verhoeff-valid), more than 5,000 transactions, an unreadable date or amount, or a credit dated more than 400 days back or more than a day ahead. **Unbroken 12-digit numbers are not refused** (they are UPI references, ADR-0042 P9): instead every digit run of 9 or more in a narration (accounts, cards, mobiles, Aadhaar-like numbers, the UTR itself) is masked to its last 4, UPI IDs and e-mails to their domain, then `redact()`; the narration is encrypted with the school's DEK and erased after 90 days (daily job). Only credits are stored; a reference column holding an account number ("TRANSFER FROM 5010…", or any all-digit reference of 9+ digits that is not a UTR) is dropped. The same credit (reference, amount, value date) of an overlapping statement is stored once; a credit without any reference may appear twice and is dismissed as `duplicate_credit`.
- **Proposals and confirmations.** Rules only propose (config `matching`): `utr_exact` (the credit's UTR is an issued, unconfirmed UPI receipt's and the amounts are equal; ticked in the review) and `amount_date` (credits **without** a UTR only: the single unconfirmed, unproposed UPI receipt of the same amount dated 3 days before to 1 day after the value date, and no other credit wanting it; not ticked). A receipt is proposed for or confirmed by one credit at a time (unique index). `POST /fees/bank-statements/{id}/confirm` (bulk) re-checks each line under lock and skips (back to unmatched) a proposal that no longer holds; confirming again counts as `already_confirmed`. `POST /fees/bank-statement-lines/{id}/confirm` matches one credit by hand (409 `amount_mismatch`, `utr_differs`, `receipt_already_confirmed`, `line_decided`), including a confirmed credit whose receipt was cancelled and issued again. `POST /fees/bank-statement-lines/{id}/dismiss` marks a credit not a fee (reason code). `POST /fees/bank-statements/rematch` proposes receipts issued after an import. No maker-checker: a confirmation states a fact and changes no money (ADR-0042 P7). The database refuses a confirmation of anything but an issued UPI receipt of the same amount and (when the bank shows one) the same UTR (trigger `upi_confirmations_check`), keeps the bank's facts and a person's decision on a line (trigger `bank_statement_lines_frozen`) and makes imports and confirmations append-only.
- **Exceptions and report (FR-FEE-017, FR-FEE-026).** `GET /fees/upi-exceptions`: issued UPI receipts dated at least `confirm_within_days` ago and not confirmed; unmatched credits with a computed reason (`no_receipt`, `amount_mismatch` with the receipt, `receipt_cancelled`, `receipt_already_confirmed`); confirmed credits whose receipt was cancelled afterwards. `GET /fees/reports/upi` (`fee.reconcile` or `finance.read`): issued UPI receipts per day, confirmed vs not yet, and how many are past the window. The receipt shows `bank_confirmed_at` ("Not yet seen in bank statement" until then). Job `fees.upi_morning` (07:00 IST) erases old narrations and notifies `fee.reconcile` holders (`fee.upi_exceptions`, counts only, once a day).
- **Audit (FR-FEE-025).** `fee.statement.read`, `fee.statement.imported` (preset, kind, counts), `fee.upi.confirmed` (counts), `fee.upi.line_ignored` (reason code); never UTRs, narrations, UPI IDs or names.

A trigger keeps what a link asks for unchangeable and lets an expired, cancelled or failed link still become `paid` (the provider is the truth); `paid` is final. Staff create links with `fee.collect` (`Idempotency-Key`); `fees.service.create_payment_intent(..., created_via="parent_page"|"reminder")` is the data contract for R2-3H/R2-3G, with `payment_link()` for the redirect.
- **Webhook (FR-PAY-007).** `POST /api/v1/webhooks/razorpay/{route}` with `require_webhook_signature("razorpay")` and `app.fees.webhook.RazorpayWebhookVerifier`: unknown or revoked route 404 before anything else; the school's current secret and, within 7 days of a rotation, the previous one; hex HMAC-SHA256 of the raw body in constant time (malformed, missing or wrong: 401 without detail; a body signed for another school's route fails); `x-razorpay-event-id` is required and is both the guard's replay key and the inbox's unique key. The stored payload is **minimised** before encryption (ids, statuses, amounts, our references; no contact, email, VPA, card or bank). If storing fails the replay claim is released (`public.guards.forget_event`) so Razorpay's retry is not mistaken for a replay.
- **Processing (FR-PAY-008, FR-PAY-009).** One event per transaction, oldest first, `SKIP LOCKED`; retries with backoff (`payments.yaml`), `failed` after 8 attempts (shown on the reconciliation screen). Every payment event re-fetches the payment and its order; checks: captured, INR, the order's receipt names one of this school's intents, amount equal to the intent's; then the account is locked (the counter's lock order: account, its links, then rows that reference a link), the receipt is re-checked under the lock, and the receipt is written in the same gap-free series (mode `razorpay`, `collected_by` NULL, collector shown as "Online payment (Razorpay)", dated the capture's IST date unless that day is closed, else today; a closed today retries the next day). Exceptions (never a receipt): `amount_mismatch` (`amount`, `currency`), `paid_not_receipted` (`dues_already_paid` when the counter took the same dues first, `intent_already_paid`, `account_closed`), `unknown_intent`, `refunded` (`refunded_before_receipt`). Unrelated payments of the school's account (no SchoolOS reference) are ignored. A later receipt for the same payment resolves its open items.
- **Refunds (FR-PAY-011, FR-FEE-013).** An online receipt is refunded only to the original method (422 `original_method_only`; `original_method` on a counter receipt: 422 `original_method_online_only`), only while connected (409 `payments_not_connected`). Approval (maker-checker, step-up) records the decision and queues `fees.send_online_refund`; the worker calls the refund API with `receipt` = the refund id (Razorpay's idempotency reference) and moves it to `processing`; `refund.processed` (or the hourly re-fetch) writes the voucher (`RFD/…`) and the `refund` ledger entries; `refund.failed` marks it failed, opens a `refund_failed` item and notifies the requester (`payments.refund_failed`).
- **Reconciliation (FR-PAY-010).** `fees.reconcile_online` at 06:00 IST (00:30 UTC) per connected school for the previous IST day: provider payments with a SchoolOS reference and no receipt (`paid_not_receipted`), receipt amount differences, refunds without a voucher (`refunded`), receipts whose payment is not captured (`receipted_not_captured`), settlements read from the settlement report and payments not settled 3 working days (Monday to Friday) after payment (`settlement_missing`). Items are unique per kind and payment, explained by `fee.manage` holders with a 10-500 character note (audited without the note) or resolved by a later receipt; `fee.manage` holders get `payments.reconciliation_exceptions` each morning there are new ones. The list is read with `finance.read` or `fee.manage` (`fee.reconcile` is R2-2E's; the lead may switch these routes to it).
- **Other jobs.** Inbox sweep every minute; expired links closed and in-flight refunds re-fetched hourly; OAuth refresh 14 days before access-token expiry (the new pair stored in the same transaction), owner alert 14 days before refresh-token expiry, inbox rows deleted 90 days after processing, daily at 03:10 IST.

*As built (R2-3H, 2026-10-18):* FR-PUB-007..011 hold for the page content: `GET /public/links/current` returns the frame plus one block for its kind (notice: title, body, date, `has_pdf`; receipt: number, date, child's name, class-section, admission number, lines, total, amount in words, mode, `cancelled_on`, `has_pdf`; dues: child's first name, class, instalments with due date, amount, late fee and overdue, totals, `pay_online`, the UPI QR and payee, the office phone; opt-in: the three categories with their state and the message language). A handle opens a page only for its pinned target type and, for receipt, dues and opt-in, while its guardian is still linked to the child; otherwise the uniform 404. `GET /public/links/current/document` gives the PDF (presigned ≤ 5 min, audited), `POST /public/links/current/pay` creates or reuses the payment link (FR-PAY-005; `public_pay` counts new links only), `GET /public/links/current/payment` gives its status for the return page, `POST /public/links/current/optin` sets the guardian's language (FR-LANG-003). The BFF adds a CSRF token bound to the cookie and an Origin check on both posts and redirects only to Razorpay's https hosts.

## 7. Constraints and assumptions

- Solo developer: managed services, modular monolith, high automation (ADR-0001).
- One codebase for both tiers; dedicated hosts are one-tenant installs with RLS still on (ADR-0015).
- No vendor portal APIs (exports only).
- AI outputs are advisory; official records change only through human workflows.

## 9. Verification approach

Each FR maps to automated tests named with its ID (e.g., `test_FR_CR_002_self_approval_forbidden`). NFR-PERF via load tests in staging (k6/Locust). NFR-SEC via CI scans, authz/RLS suites, ZAP baseline, and external pen test. RAG requirements via the eval harness (06 §13) as CI gates.
