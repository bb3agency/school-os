## How {{school_short_name}} uses information about you in SchoolOS

Effective {{effective_date}} (notice version {{template_version}}).

| What | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Your name, e-mail, phone and role | To give you a SchoolOS account with the right access | While you work here, then as the school's records require |
| Sign-in records and an audit log of what you do with student and school records | Security and accountability; the law requires security logs | At least 1 year; audit archives 3 years |
| Your questions to "Ask the school" and the answers | To answer you; kept as the record of AI use | 180 days |
| Your Ask memory (preferences you confirm) | To help you get better answers; you can turn it off or delete it | Until you delete it or leave the school |

This information is never used to evaluate or monitor you as an employee beyond security and accountability. SchoolOS staff cannot see it without the school's approval.

**Your rights** are the same as any data principal's: access, correction, erasure where lawful, nomination, withdrawal of consent and complaint. Contact {{privacy_contact_name}} · {{privacy_contact_phone}} · {{privacy_contact_email}}. We aim to answer within 7 days. You may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

**Your duty:** student and family information you see in SchoolOS is confidential. Do not copy it into personal phones, chats or other apps, and do not upload Aadhaar card images.
