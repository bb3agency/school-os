## Context

docs/18 §3 R2.3 adds parent messages. The office today copies approved notices (M4) into parents' WhatsApp groups by hand; teachers' personal numbers are exposed and there is no record of who was told what. Owner decision **D6**: WhatsApp only (no SMS, no TRAI DLT), the **Meta WhatsApp Cloud API directly** (no BSP), behind a provider interface. **D7**: each school uses its own WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) and number and **pays Meta directly** in INR; SchoolOS charges nothing for messages (ADR-0038 unchanged).

Facts checked on 2026-10-14 (sources at the end):

- **Onboarding.** Embedded Signup (Facebook Login for Business + JavaScript SDK) lets a business create or pick its business portfolio, WABA and phone number and grant the partner's app access. It returns the WABA id, the phone number id and a code the partner's server exchanges for a **business token** scoped to that customer. The partner then registers the number for Cloud API use and subscribes its app to the WABA's webhooks. **Tech Providers** use business tokens only and their customers add **their own payment method** to the WABA (Solution Partners share a credit line instead). Onboarding needs advanced access to the WhatsApp permissions through App Review; by default 10 new customers per rolling 7 days, 200 after Business Verification, App Review and Access Verification. Embedded Signup **v2 is deprecated on 15 Oct 2026**; v4 is current.
- **Pricing.** Since 1 Jul 2025 Meta charges per delivered template message by category and recipient country. India utility ≈ ₹0.115 + 18% GST per message (research notes §1; Meta's INR rate card was not retrieved). Non-template messages and utility templates sent inside an open 24-hour customer service window (opened by the user messaging the business) are free. Indian WABAs must move to **INR billing by 31 Dec 2026**; from 1 Jan 2027 Meta stops delivering messages from non-INR WABAs of eligible customers.
- **Categories.** A utility template must be non-promotional and specific to the user (account, service, transaction) or essential (safety). Mixed or unclear content is classified marketing; since 9 Apr 2025 a template submitted as utility but judged marketing is approved as marketing; Meta may recategorise later (webhook `template_category_update`, normally one day's notice). Confirming an opt-in collected elsewhere is a utility use case.
- **Limits.** New business portfolios may message 250 unique users per moving 24 hours outside service windows; 2,000 after a scaling path (business verification, or 2,000 deliveries in 30 days with high quality), then 10,000, 100,000 and unlimited by automatic scaling. Limits are per business portfolio.
- **Webhooks.** One callback URL per app (with per-WABA or per-number overrides); `GET` verification with `hub.mode`, `hub.challenge`, `hub.verify_token`; payloads signed with `X-Hub-Signature-256: sha256=<HMAC-SHA256 of the raw body with the app secret>`; retried for up to 7 days, so duplicates happen; payloads up to 3 MB. mTLS is available per app.
- **Policy.** The WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy requires opt-in permission before contacting a person, makes the business responsible for the opt-in method, requires honouring every opt-out request, and recommends separate opt-in per message category.

## Decision

1. **WhatsApp only, Meta Cloud API direct, behind an interface (owner D6).** New module `app/messaging`. `app/messaging/providers/` defines `MessagingProvider` (exchange a signup code, register a number, subscribe the app, create and list templates, send a template, send a free-form text inside an open service window, verify and parse webhooks) with implementations `meta_cloud` and `fake` (local and CI; refused in staging and prod). Only `app/messaging/providers/*` calls Meta (import-linter contract, egress allowlist `graph.facebook.com`). A BSP can be added later as another implementation with an ADR amendment. No SMS and no DLT.
2. **The school's own WABA and number; the school pays Meta (owner D7).** Each school connects its own WABA and phone number; messages go out under the school's display name. The school adds its own payment method and INR currency in Meta's Billing Hub; SchoolOS shows the INR-migration status and refuses to send from an account not confirmed as INR after 31 Dec 2026. SchoolOS never shares a credit line and never resells messages.
3. **Utility templates only, for four purposes (owner D6; invariant 14).** Business-initiated messages are approved **utility** templates from a versioned catalogue (`app/messaging/templates.yaml`): `optin_confirm`, `school_notice` (an approved M4 notice), `absence_alert`, `fee_reminder`, `fee_receipt` (and `optin_stopped`, sent free inside the window). Marketing templates are never created; admissions, events for sale, summer camps and anything promotional are refused at the catalogue level. A template Meta moves to marketing is **paused automatically** and the school is told.
4. **Every message links to a short-lived parent page, never a login (owner D6; ADR-0046).** Templates carry minimal text and a URL button whose dynamic suffix is a parent link token; the page shows the notice, receipt or dues. Parents never sign in.
5. **(P) Onboarding as a Meta Tech Provider with Embedded Signup v4.** SchoolOS registers one Meta app (business-verified, App Review for `whatsapp_business_management` and `whatsapp_business_messaging`), becomes a Tech Provider, and runs Embedded Signup v4 from the school's "WhatsApp" settings page (owner, step-up). The server exchanges the code for the school's business token, registers the number (two-step PIN generated by SchoolOS, stored wrapped), subscribes the app to the WABA, and creates the catalogue's templates in the school's WABA. The business token is a per-school secret wrapped with `core.crypto.wrap_bound` (`resource: "messaging_account/<id>"`); the Meta app secret, app id and webhook verify token are platform secrets in Secrets Manager (docs/10 §11.2). For a pilot before App Review, an operator MAY help the school create a system-user token in its own Business Manager and paste it (`mode = manual_token`), same wrapping, same checks.
6. **(P) One webhook endpoint, routed by WABA id.** `GET/POST /api/v1/webhooks/meta`. `GET` answers the challenge only when `hub.verify_token` matches (`require_meta_verify_token()`). `POST` is verified with `require_webhook_signature("meta")` (app secret, raw body, constant time, ≤ 3 MB), then each `entry[].id` (WABA id) is resolved to a school through the public handle resolver (ADR-0047); an unknown WABA is dropped with a counter. Events go to the school's inbox `msg.provider_events` (encrypted, unique on the event key) and a worker applies them: message statuses (`sent`, `delivered`, `read`, `failed` with code), inbound messages (keywords only), template status and category updates, phone-number quality and account updates.
7. **(P) Opt-in per guardian and category, with evidence.** Categories `notices`, `attendance`, `fees`. Opt-in methods, in order of strength: (a) the parent sends a join message to the school number from a printed slip or QR (`wa.me/<school number>?text=JOIN <code>`), which also opens a free service window for the confirmation; (b) a signed admission or consent form uploaded as an evidence document; (c) recorded in person by staff (method `in_person`, the recorder named). Every decision is an append-only row with wording version, method, evidence and who recorded it. Nothing is sent to a guardian without a current opt-in for that category. Opt-out: the inbound keywords `STOP`, `UNSUBSCRIBE` (and Telugu `ఆపు` when the school's parent language is Telugu) or the template's "Stop messages" quick reply opt the number out of every category at once, confirmed by a free text reply; staff can record an opt-out too. Re-joining needs a new opt-in.
8. **(P) Phone numbers.** The guardian's phone stays where it is (`sis.guardians`, C3, encrypted, blind index). Sending decrypts it inside the worker only to call Meta (`91` + 10 digits); message rows store the guardian id and the phone's blind index, never the number. Inbound `wa_id`s are matched by blind index. No phone number, name or message text appears in logs, traces, metrics or audit summaries (invariant 5).
9. **(P) No inbox.** SchoolOS does not offer two-way chat in R2. Inbound text other than keywords gets one free reply per day inside the window ("This number sends school notices. For questions call the office at <office phone>.") and is **not stored** (only kind and time). Staff cannot type free-form messages to parents.
10. **(P) Sending rules.** A per-school queue with a token bucket below Meta's throughput, quiet hours 21:00-07:00 IST (absence alerts and receipts excepted until 20:00), at most one absence alert per student per day and one fee reminder per account per offset, a daily cap per school (default the messaging limit minus 10%). Before a notice broadcast the sender sees the number of recipients, how many will be skipped (no opt-in, no phone, opted out) and the estimated Meta cost (count × the configured utility rate + GST), and confirms. Transient errors retry with backoff (3 attempts within an hour); permanent errors (for example undeliverable or not a WhatsApp user) are recorded and shown in the delivery report. A `FLAGGED` or `RED` quality rating, an account restriction or a paused template stops new sends for that school until a person resumes.
11. **(P) Absence alerts** are generated from attendance marks (M5) after a school-set time (default 11:00 IST), only for `absent` (not `late` or `leave`), only for opted-in guardians, and never for a correction after the alert went out (a later correction sends nothing; the alert says "marked absent at the time of sending"). The message carries the child's first name and the date only.
12. **(P) Dedicated tier.** Meta's single callback per app and the app secret cannot be given to dedicated hosts (the app secret also mints app access tokens). In R2 WhatsApp messages are offered on the shared tier only; a dedicated school keeps copying notices. A signed relay that forwards only that school's events to its host is a follow-up (owner question).
13. **Audit and accountability.** Connecting or disconnecting an account, template creation and status changes, every opt-in and opt-out, every broadcast (who, which notice, how many recipients, skipped counts), every individual send request and its final status are audited in the transaction that records them (ids, codes and counts only).

## Consequences

- Good: an official channel from the school's own number with opt-in, opt-out, delivery status and an audit trail; no teacher numbers exposed; costs stay with the school at Meta's utility rate (about ₹800 a month for 1,000 students at six messages each).
- Good: no per-message margin for SchoolOS to explain (D7), and the provider interface keeps a BSP option open.
- Bad / costs: Tech Provider status (business verification, App Review) is a dependency before the first school; a new school starts at 250 unique recipients a day, so the onboarding checklist must include Meta business verification; each language variant of each template needs Meta's approval per WABA.
- Bad / costs: Meta may recategorise templates or restrict an account; sends then stop until a person acts.
- Follow-up: privacy review (Meta is the school's chosen provider; SchoolOS transmits on the school's instruction, docs/08 §15), DPA wording, a native Telugu review of the Telugu templates, the dedicated-tier relay.

## Alternatives considered

| Option | Why not chosen |
|---|---|
| A BSP (Gupshup, Interakt, AiSensy, Wati) | Monthly fees of ₹1,000-3,800 per school and markups; owner chose Meta direct (D6) |
| One shared SchoolOS number for all schools | Blurs whose communication it is under DPDP; one school's complaints would lower every school's quality rating |
| SMS fallback | Needs TRAI DLT registration per school; owner chose WhatsApp only (D6) |
| Solution Partner with a shared credit line | SchoolOS would carry schools' message costs and credit risk; contradicts D7 |
| Two-way chat inbox for the office | Large scope, staff workload and moderation; out of R2 |
| Store message text | Needed only to show "what was sent"; the template version and encrypted variables are enough and expire |
