# Regulatory and government-portal obligations of Indian private schools (Andhra Pradesh focus), 2025-2026

Research date: 2026-10-10. About 15 tool calls. Most sources are secondary (news, coaching sites, teacher blogs). Official PDFs (UDISE+ manuals, BSEAP press notes, CBSE circulars, the DPDP gazette) were not fetched directly. Treat dates as indicative and verify them against the primary documents before building deadline logic.

## 1. UDISE+ student data (SDMS / Student Module): fields, Aadhaar validation, deadlines, rejections, who does it

### Takeaway
UDISE+ student records are one record per child, keyed by an 11-digit PEN. Aadhaar validation fails unless name, gender and DOB in the General Profile match UIDAI exactly, down to spelling and spacing. Schools usually cannot fix name or DOB themselves; the correction goes to the Block/District MIS coordinator. The 2026-27 student-data freeze reportedly fell on 30 Sep 2026, which has passed. Duplicate records and "stuck" records at the previous school are the main operational pain points.

### Cited Findings
- Aadhaar validation fails when the student's name, gender or DOB in the UDISE+ General Profile (GP) does not match the UIDAI database exactly, down to spelling and spacing. The school must check the original Aadhaar card, edit the GP character for character, and click "Validate Aadhaar from UIDAI". — [educationforallinindia (UDISE+ 2026-27 FAQ)](https://educationforallinindia.com/udise-2026-27-answers-to-the-questions-parents-teachers-and-school-heads-are-actually-asking/); [gstmunshi UDISE+ student module guide](https://gstmunshi.com/blog/udise-plus-student-module-progression-pen-login-guide)
- Aadhaar authentication runs through the school's login in the Student Module, not through a parent-facing portal. — [educationforallinindia](https://educationforallinindia.com/udise-2026-27-answers-to-the-questions-parents-teachers-and-school-heads-are-actually-asking/)
- Schools generally cannot change name or DOB from their own login. The request goes to the Block or District MIS coordinator, who verifies and approves it. — [educationforallinindia](https://educationforallinindia.com/udise-2026-27-answers-to-the-questions-parents-teachers-and-school-heads-are-actually-asking/); [udise.net student data entry guide](https://udise.net/udise-student-data-entry/)
- One guide says a student's name can be corrected only once, and only for students enrolled in the current academic year. This needs verification. — [teluguvidya: Student name correction in UDISE Plus 2025-26](https://teluguvidya.com/student-name-correction-in-udise-plus/)
- The PEN is an 11-digit permanent student identifier, unchanged by APAAR. — [gstmunshi](https://gstmunshi.com/blog/udise-plus-student-module-progression-pen-login-guide); [anantamias](https://anantamias.com/udise-plus/)
- On transfers, the new school should import the record by PEN, after searching by name and DOB first. Creating a fresh record can leave the child "active" in two schools. The previous school must release the active record, with inter-state cases escalated through State MIS. — [educationforallinindia UDISE+ 2025-26 data analysis](https://educationforallinindia.com/?p=25804); [anantamias](https://anantamias.com/udise-plus/)
- Several sources give 30 September 2026 as the 2026-27 student-data freeze or deadline. — [Circin blog](https://circin.ai/blog/udise-plus-2026-27-deadline-apaar-guide); [classegy compliance guide](https://classegy.com/resources/udise-plus-compliance-guide-2026-27)
- APAAR IDs are generated by UDISE+ against the child's PEN and fail when the name does not match Aadhaar. Fix the mismatch in UDISE+ first. — [Circin](https://circin.ai/blog/udise-plus-2026-27-deadline-apaar-guide)
- The UDISE+ Student Module is also where admissions, progression and transfer-out (TC) movements are recorded. — [anantamias](https://anantamias.com/udise-plus/); [gimkit UDISE student module guide](https://gimkit.it.com/news/?p=847)

### Inferences
- The highest-value SchoolOS feature is a pre-submission "Aadhaar-as-printed vs school record" diff. It should do exact string comparison including spacing and initials, plus DOB and gender, and flag rows before the clerk tries "Validate from UIDAI". This maps directly to SchoolOS's existing per-source attribute model and dq_findings.
- A transfer-in workflow should prompt "search by PEN / name+DOB, do not create new". It should also track pending release requests to the previous school.
- Because name corrections are limited (possibly to one) and go through MIS approval, SchoolOS should block casual edits. It should route a change through maker-checker with evidence, which matches the existing `changes` module.
- **Conflict with CLAUDE.md invariant 4:** UDISE+ itself holds Aadhaar, and the school enters it. SchoolOS stores only last-4 plus as-printed demographics. That is fine for pre-checks, because the clerk keys the full number into UDISE+ directly. SchoolOS cannot automate the Aadhaar entry step, and should say so in its marketing.

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve the official UDISE+ 2024-25/2025-26 SDMS user manual or Data Capture Format, so the exact mandatory field list is not confirmed from a primary source. From memory and unverified, it includes: GP (name, gender, DOB, mother's and father's names, Aadhaar and name-as-per-Aadhaar, address, mobile, social category, minority, BPL, disability (CWSN), mother tongue, blood group), Enrolment Profile (admission number, class, section, medium, roll number), and Facility Profile (incentives, scholarships). Verify all of this on udiseplus.gov.in.
- No official deadline found for the 2024-25 or 2025-26 cycles. No official data on national rejection rates.
- Who does it: secondary sources imply the school HM/office clerk logs in, with Block MIS coordinators approving. No AP order assigning the role was found.

## 2. APAAR ID: generation, consent, Aadhaar matching, failures, AP status, targets, DigiLocker

### Takeaway
APAAR is generated through UDISE+ against PEN, with "name as per Aadhaar" as a mandatory field. It needs parental consent, which the Supreme Court (20 Jul 2026) ordered must include an explicit option to refuse. It is officially voluntary. CBSE nevertheless made it mandatory for registration and LOC from 2026-27, and fewer than 50% of CBSE students had one in 2025-26. The dominant failure mode is a name or DOB mismatch with Aadhaar.

### Cited Findings
- The official APAAR portal lists parental consent as a step before generation and "Name as per Aadhaar" as a mandatory field. — [apaar.education.gov.in](https://apaar.education.gov.in/)
- Where the error is in the school's UDISE+ record, the school corrects it. Where the error is in Aadhaar, it must be fixed at an Aadhaar centre first. — [chitti.app APAAR guide](https://blog.chitti.app/apaar-id-school-students-why-schools-ask/); [learnsync](https://learnsync.in/blog/what-is-apaar-id)
- PEN and valid Aadhaar are prerequisites in several states. For example, Assam generates only for students with valid Aadhaar and PEN. — [Careers360 (Assam)](https://news.careers360.com/assam-announces-apaar-id-rollout-digital-identity-for-students-to-enhance-academic-tracking); [databus](https://databus.co/blog/apaar-id-for-students/)
- **Supreme Court, *Abhishek Baxi & Ors. v. Union of India*, WP(C) 832/2026, order of 20 Jul 2026:**
  - The model APAAR consent form is to be amended pan-India to let parents expressly withhold or refuse consent, following the Orissa HC in *Rohit Anand Das v. State of Odisha*.
  - All APAAR data handling must comply with the DPDP Act 2023.
  - Data may not be shared with private entities except as permitted by law.
  - The scheme was not struck down.
  - Source: secondary summary, not the order itself. — [educationforallinindia, SC on APAAR (Jul 2026)](https://educationforallinindia.com/supreme-court-on-apaar-what-parents-and-schools-need-to-know-july-2026/)
- The same page cites three circulars:
  - MoE circular of 11 Oct 2023, which required consent but whose form did not clearly allow refusal;
  - CBSE circular of 5 Aug 2025, making APAAR mandatory for Class IX-XII board registration from 2026;
  - CBSE circular of 27 Aug 2025.
  - — [educationforallinindia](https://educationforallinindia.com/supreme-court-on-apaar-what-parents-and-schools-need-to-know-july-2026/)
- Petitioners alleged that some schools tied APAAR or Aadhaar compliance to admission or exam registration. This is a claim, not a finding. — [same](https://educationforallinindia.com/supreme-court-on-apaar-what-parents-and-schools-need-to-know-july-2026/)
- An NCERT clarification reportedly says APAAR is not mandatory for school admissions, and the consent form was revised to include a refusal option. — [educationforallinindia, APAAR not mandatory for admissions](https://educationforallinindia.com/apaar-id-not-mandatory-for-school-admissions-ncert-clarification-parental-hardships-court-victory-revised-consent-form/)
- CBSE governing body minutes (reported 19 Feb 2026) make APAAR a mandatory field for registration and LOC from 2026-27, and link APAAR creation with the registration and LOC modules for Classes 9-12. Fewer than 50% of students in affiliated schools had APAAR in 2025-26. — [Careers360, 19 Feb 2026](https://news.careers360.com/cbse-board-exams-apaar-id-class-9-11-loc-registration-2026-27-two-level-10-maths-social-science-2028/amp)

### Inferences
- SchoolOS needs a consent register for APAAR with three states: given, refused, pending. It also needs the revised form with a refusal option, a printable bilingual (English for now) consent slip, and a dated evidence record. It must not present APAAR as an admission precondition.
- APAAR failure lists should be generated from the same Aadhaar-diff engine as UDISE+ (section 1), so one fix clears both.
- There is tension between CBSE's "mandatory for LOC" and SC/NCERT's "voluntary". SchoolOS should surface the students who refused consent, so the school can follow the board's exception process. It should not force generation.

### Gaps
- No AP-specific APAAR generation statistics, targets or circulars were found for 2025-26.
- The DigiLocker linkage mechanics (APAAR credentials pushed to DigiLocker) were not confirmed from a source in this session.
- I did not retrieve a source for an official national APAAR target or deadline for 2025-26.

## 3. Andhra Pradesh specifics: SSC nominal rolls, Child Info / cse.ap.gov.in, LEAP, fee regulation, RTE 12(1)(c), TC and registers

### Takeaway
**SSC nominal rolls.** For the March 2026 SSC exams, BSEAP took the nominal-roll data from UDISE+ for the first time. Corrections had to be made in UDISE+ and were accepted only where they matched Aadhaar. 3,497 schools requested corrections for 9,986 students. This makes UDISE+ and Aadhaar alignment in Class 9 and 10 the single most important AP pain point.

**RTE.** RTE 25% admissions run on a cse.ap.gov.in lottery with proximity-based selection.

**Fees and attendance.**
- AP fee caps (GO 53/54, 2021-24) were struck down by the AP High Court, and no current school fee GO was found.
- Face-recognition attendance applies to government teachers. I found no source on private schools.

### Cited Findings
**SSC (Class 10) nominal rolls (BSEAP)**
- For March 2026, the board sourced nominal-roll data from the UDISE+ portal for the first time. Heads of schools were told to verify surname, name and DOB against school records, with a correction window open to 31 Mar 2026. — [LatestLY (via search summary)](https://www.latestly.com/india/education/ap-ssc-hall-ticket-2026-released-at-bse-ap-gov-in-heres-how-to-download-7337557.html)

  - Correction window on UDISE+ ran 26-28 Feb 2026, with exams from 16 Mar 2026, and a final opportunity in the last week of March or first week of April 2026.

  - Requests based on school records alone, without Aadhaar verification, would not be entertained. If school records and Aadhaar differ, students should update Aadhaar.
  - Requests came from 3,497 schools in 614 mandals, covering 9,986 unique students. The Head of Institution is responsible for discrepancies.

## 4. CBSE: registration (Class 9/11), LOC (10/12), corrections, APAAR, affiliation bye-laws disclosure

### Takeaway
CBSE registration for Classes 9 and 11 runs on Pariksha Sangam and is a precondition for board exams. For the first time, schools had to link APAAR IDs in 2025-26, and APAAR becomes a mandatory field for registration and LOC from 2026-27. Correction windows are short and never extended. Mandatory Public Disclosure (Appendix IX) on the school website must include the full fee structure and other items. CBSE set a 15 Feb 2026 deadline to update disclosures.

## 5. DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025: children's data, consent, education exemption, timeline, fiduciary vs processor, breach, retention

### Takeaway
The DPDP Rules were notified on 13 Nov 2025 (G.S.R. 846(E)/843(E)), with phased commencement. Most obligations apply after 18 months, around 13-14 May 2027. These include notice, consent, security, breach reporting, children's verifiable parental consent (s.9 and rule 10), and retention. Consent-manager provisions start at 12 months (Nov 2026). Under the Fourth Schedule, educational institutions are exempt from verifiable parental consent and from the tracking/monitoring ban, but only for educational activities and child safety. Schools are data fiduciaries; SaaS vendors are processors. Breach reporting is two-stage: immediate intimation, then a detailed report within 72 hours, with no harm threshold.

### Cited Findings
- **Notification and phasing:**
  - Notified 13 Nov 2025, with an 18-month, three-stage commencement. Children's consent (s.9, rule 10) falls in the 18-month tranche.
  - Sources differ between 13 May and 14 May 2027 for the 18-month date.
  - Consent managers come in at 12 months.
  - — [ruleexpert](https://ruleexpert.com/guides/dpdp-rules-2025/); [EY](https://www.ey.com/en_in/insights/cybersecurity/transforming-data-privacy-digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025); [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/digital/breaking-govt-notifies-final-dpdp-rules-sets-staggered-rollout-with-key-obligations-for-data-fiduciaries-84201.htm); [KPMG](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmgsites/in/pdf/2025/11/dpdp-rules-2025-guidance-to-dpdp-act-implementation.pdf)
- **Who is a child:** anyone under 18, with no graduated band. — [vratex](https://www.vratex.com/dpdp-act/childrens-data); [ORF](https://www.orfonline.org/expert-speak/dpdp-rules-and-the-future-of-child-data-safety)
- **Rule 10 verifiable parental consent:**
  - accepted methods are reliable identity and age details already held, a virtual token from an authorised entity, or a DigiLocker credential;
  - a self-declared checkbox is insufficient;
  - the children's provisions also bar profiling, behavioural monitoring and targeted advertising.
  - — [consently](https://www.consently.in/blog/verifiable-parental-consent-dpdp-rules-2025-edtech-gaming); [sansalegal](https://www.sansalegal.com/post/how-to-design-a-verifiable-parental-consent-process-under-the-dpdp-rules-2025)
- **Fourth Schedule exemption:** educational institutions, clinical establishments, crèches and school transport are exempt from verifiable parental consent and the tracking ban, for those purposes only (educational activities, safety of enrolled children). It is unclear whether EdTech or private tutors qualify. — [raysolute DPDPA guide for schools](https://www.raysolute.com/dpdpa-compliance-guide-schools.html); [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2025/11/223-dpdp-rules-tracking-children-parental-consent/); [privacyglobal](https://www.privacyglobal.org/blog/dpdp-rules-need-to-know)
- **Breach reporting:**
  - intimate the Board without delay, then send a detailed report within 72 hours (the Board can extend);
  - notify affected individuals without delay;
  - no harm threshold applies.
  - — [amlegals](https://amlegals.com/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025/); [Seclore](https://www.seclore.com/fundamentals/dpdp-rules-2025-compliance-guide/); [lawrbit](https://lawrbit.com/article/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025)
- **Retention:**
  - The three-year inactivity erasure (with 48-hour notice) applies only to large e-commerce, gaming and social media entities, not schools.
  - All fiduciaries must keep personal data, traffic data and logs for at least 1 year for specified purposes.
  - A fiduciary must also cause its processors to erase data.
  - — [Mondaq](https://webiis08.mondaq.com/india/data-protection/1706890/a-closer-look-at-the-dpdp-rules-2025); [amlegals](https://amlegals.com/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025/)
- The SC APAAR order (20 Jul 2026) applies the DPDP Act to APAAR data handling. — [educationforallinindia](https://educationforallinindia.com/supreme-court-on-apaar-what-parents-and-schools-need-to-know-july-2026/)

### Inferences
- **Roles:** schools are data fiduciaries. SchoolOS, as a managed SaaS, is a processor and needs a processor agreement (DPA) with each school. SchoolOS can offer the school's compliance tooling:
  - a breach runbook supporting the 72-hour report;
  - a consent register;
  - a retention policy per record class;
  - a data-principal request workflow;
  - one-year log retention.
- **Product guardrail:** the exemption covers only education and child safety. Analytics, insights or marketing on student data outside those purposes would need verifiable parental consent and may breach the tracking ban. This aligns with CLAUDE.md invariant 14 (M5 purpose limit).
- **Readiness deadline:** about May 2027 is a selling point for the 2027-28 academic year.

## 6. Aadhaar collection by schools (Puttaswamy, UIDAI), masked Aadhaar

### Takeaway
Under the 2018 Puttaswamy (Aadhaar) judgment, Aadhaar cannot be made mandatory for school admission, because admission is neither a subsidy nor a service under s.7 of the Aadhaar Act. UIDAI has told schools not to deny admission for lack of Aadhaar. Even so, UDISE+, APAAR and BSEAP corrections in practice depend on Aadhaar matching. Schools therefore collect it "voluntarily" for portal validation.

### Cited Findings
- The SC (2018) held that Aadhaar is not required for school admissions, since admission is not a subsidy or service under s.7, and that mandating it would violate Art. 21. — [LatestLY](https://www.latestly.com/india/aadhaar-not-needed-for-school-admissions-cbse-ugc-neet-exams-says-supreme-court-404187.html); [SFLC.in FAQ](https://sflc.in/faqs-aadhaar/)
- UIDAI (Dec 2018) said schools should not insist on Aadhaar, and no student should be denied admission on this ground. — [Deccan Chronicle via PressReader](https://www.pressreader.com/india/deccan-chronicle/20181226/282437055237355)
- The Delhi HC (2023) upheld the stay on Delhi circulars that mandated Aadhaar for EWS/DG/CWSN private-school admissions. — [Verdictum](https://verdictum.in/court-updates/high-courts/delhi-hc-upholds-suspension-of-circulars-making-aadhaar-mandatory-requirement-for-ewsdgcwsn-pvt-school-admissions-1495991); [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2023/09/223-delhi-high-court-government-circulars-aadhaar-school-admission/)
- AP RTE admissions list Aadhaar among the verification documents. — [karmasandhan](https://www.karmasandhan.com/ap-rte-lottery-result-phase-1/)

### Inferences
- SchoolOS's design (last-4 plus as-printed demographic fields, Verhoeff masking) fits the legal position. The clerk compares the physical card to the record and enters the full number only into government portals. The product should also support students with no Aadhaar, where a birth certificate is the evidence.

### Gaps
- No UIDAI circular on masked Aadhaar for schools, and no Aadhaar (Authentication) Regulations text on storage limits, were retrieved. Verify Aadhaar Act s.29 and the UIDAI masked-Aadhaar guidance directly.
