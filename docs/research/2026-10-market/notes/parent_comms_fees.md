# Parent communication and fee collection in Indian private schools (2025-2026)

Research date: 2026-10-10. About 20 searches and fetches. Many sources are vendor blogs with a commercial interest, and each is flagged where it is cited. Several questions (AP-specific fee practice, adoption of parent apps, ERP revenue from payments) have little independent evidence, so they are listed under Gaps rather than filled in.

## 1. WhatsApp Business Platform in India: pricing, rules, BSPs, school use

### Takeaway
Since 1 July 2025 Meta charges per delivered template message. India rates, excluding GST, are about Rs 0.86 for marketing (from 1 Jan 2026, up from about Rs 0.78) and about Rs 0.115 for utility and authentication. Free-form "service" replies are free inside the 24-hour customer service window. School use cases (fee reminders, absence alerts, results, circulars) fit the cheap utility category, provided templates contain no promotional content. Budget about Rs 0.14 per utility message including GST, plus a BSP subscription of roughly Rs 1,000-3,800 a month, or nothing if SchoolOS uses the Meta Cloud API directly. India billing must move to INR by 31 Dec 2026.

### Cited Findings
- From 1 July 2025 Meta moved "from conversation-based pricing to per-message pricing". It charges only when a template message is delivered. Rates vary by template category (marketing, utility, authentication), volume tier and recipient country — [Meta developer docs: Pricing](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing.md)
- Non-template messages are free but can only be sent inside an open 24-hour customer service window (CSW), which opens when the user messages the business. Utility templates sent inside an open CSW are free and do not count toward volume tiers — [Meta developer docs](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing.md)
- Volume tiers lower utility and authentication rates. Volume is aggregated across all WABAs in a business portfolio per market and category, and tiers reset monthly. Marketing gets no volume discount — [Meta developer docs](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing.md)
- Meta's change log lists a higher India marketing rate effective 1 Jan 2026 and a higher India "authentication-international" rate effective 1 Apr 2026. It lists no change to India utility rates in 2026 — [Meta developer docs](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing.md)
- India rates excluding GST: marketing about Rs 0.7846 before 1 Jan 2026 and Rs 0.8631 from 1 Jan 2026 (about USD 0.0107); utility about Rs 0.115; authentication about Rs 0.115 (both unchanged). 18% GST applies to Meta charges and BSP fees, so a marketing message costs about Rs 1.02 including GST — [Monty Mobile blog](https://montymobile.com/blogs/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-in-india-inr-rates-gst-and-the-2026-currency-migration-deadline). Another aggregator gives "Marketing Rs 0.86, Utility Rs 0.13, Authentication Rs 0.13" for July 2025, which appears to mix periods — [MyOperator KB](https://support.myoperator.com/portal/en/kb/articles/whatsapp-has-shifted-to-per-message-pricing-effective-july-1-2025-based-on-message-categories-and-your-recipient-s-country-in-india-there-are-three-paid-categories). Meta's own INR rate card was not retrieved.
- INR billing localisation launched on 1 Jan 2026 for customers whose Sold-To country is India. All WABAs must migrate to INR by 31 Dec 2026, and from 1 Jan 2027 Meta stops delivering messages from non-INR WABAs of eligible customers. Currency migration APIs have existed since 1 June 2026 — [Meta developer docs](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing.md)
- CONFLICT: a BSP blog says that from 1 Oct 2026 "replies beyond 1,000 free per number per month are charged, as are utility templates sent inside an open 24-hour window" and that India list rates did not change — [baat.ai](https://baat.ai/blog/whatsapp-api-pricing-india). The Meta pricing page as fetched still describes utility inside the CSW as free. Verify on Meta's page before modelling costs.
- Template categories: a utility template must be non-promotional and either specific to or requested by the user (order, account, service, transaction) or essential to the user, such as a safety alert. "Any template that has a mix of utility and marketing content will be classified as a marketing template." Meta decides the category from the exact content and can recategorise a template — [Meta template categorization](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/templates/template-categorization). Businesses are charged at the category applied at the time of use — [Meta pricing docs](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing.md)
- Opt-in: Meta lists "confirm opt-in for receiving messages on WhatsApp as a follow-up to opt-in collected via other channels" as a utility use case — [Meta template categorization](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/templates/template-categorization). A vendor summary says consent under the DPDP Act must be clear, informed and specific with no pre-checked boxes, that withdrawal must be honoured, and that DPDP enforcement is phased through May 2027. It also says WhatsApp sits outside TRAI DLT — [Monty Mobile](https://montymobile.com/blogs/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-in-india-inr-rates-gst-and-the-2026-currency-migration-deadline). The full wording of Meta's opt-in policy was not retrieved (see Gaps).
- BSP subscriptions in INR, from competitor and vendor comparisons that need verification: AiSensy Basic Rs 1,500 and Pro Rs 3,200 a month, with unlimited users and a reported 20% markup on marketing messages; Interakt Starter Rs 999, Growth Rs 2,799 and Advanced Rs 3,799 a month, with a chatbot builder costing Rs 1,000-3,000 extra; Wati Growth Rs 2,199-2,499 a month, extra agent seats Rs 1,299 each, and a reported 20% markup — [WhatsApp BSP comparison search results: wanotifier](https://wanotifier.com/compare/aisensy-vs-interakt/), [AiSensy vs Wati](https://aisensy.com/aisensy-vs-wati), [go4whatsup](https://go4whatsup.com/blog/aisensy-vs-wati-vs-interakt-pricing-showdown-2026/)
- Meta's per-message rate is the same whichever BSP is used. What differs is the platform subscription or markup the BSP adds — [baat.ai](https://baat.ai/blog/whatsapp-api-pricing-india); [MyOperator](https://support.myoperator.com/portal/en/kb/articles/whatsapp-has-shifted-to-per-message-pricing-effective-july-1-2025-based-on-message-categories-and-your-recipient-s-country-in-india-there-are-three-paid-categories)
- K-12 use cases, from a vendor blog (RichAutomate, May 2026): daily absence alerts to both parents, homework, exam schedules and result cards, fee reminders at D-7, D-1, D+0 and D+7 with UPI links, PTM booking, bus tracking, emergency broadcasts and admission enquiries. It prices utility at Rs 0.115 and marketing at Rs 0.96. It claims circular read rates rise from 22% to 96% and fee outstanding falls from 42 to 12 days after the due date. These claims are unsourced and come from two unnamed client schools, so treat them as marketing. It also captures parent language preference, Telugu included — [RichAutomate blog](https://richautomate.in/blog/whatsapp-k12-schools-india-2026)
- Problems with informal WhatsApp groups. No India-specific study was found, and these are UK and UAE sources. Inaccurate information spreads quickly, classroom disputes spill into the chats, messages meant for one parent are sent to a group, and the school cannot control onward sharing of pupil data and images — [IRIS (UK)](https://www.iris.co.uk/blog/misc/whats-up-with-whatsapp/), [Edapt (UK)](https://www.edapt.org.uk/support/knowledge-base/staff-whatsapp-groups-in-school/). UAE schools have set group rules (no posts after 8pm, teachers as admins), and some issue official SIMs so teachers do not use personal numbers — [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/education/school-whatsapp-group-policies-setting-ground-rules). An Indian vendor commentary says groups bury important messages under chatter and leave no proper record — [CampX](https://campx.in/xpress/articles/parent-school-communication-digital)

### Inferences
- A school of 1,000 students sending about 6 utility messages per student per month (absence alerts, fee reminders, circulars) would pay roughly 6,000 x Rs 0.136, about Rs 800 a month to Meta including GST. That is small compared with the BSP subscription, so per-message cost is not the barrier. The barriers are template discipline (keeping fee and attendance templates in utility) and collecting and recording opt-ins.
- Circulars are utility only if they are non-promotional and essential or account-specific. Admissions and summer-camp messages are marketing, at 7-8x the price.
- To build messaging, SchoolOS can either act as a Tech Provider on the Meta Cloud API, with one WABA per school or a shared one, or integrate one BSP (Gupshup, Interakt and others). Per-school WABAs and phone numbers keep the sender identity as the school's own and fit the dedicated tier. A shared number is cheaper but blurs whose communication it is under DPDP.
- The main problem with informal groups is that teachers' personal numbers are exposed and there is no audit trail. An official, school-number-based channel with an audit record fits SchoolOS invariant 7 (audit) and the privacy posture of invariant 5.

### Gaps
- Meta's official INR rate card (CSV) and India volume-tier thresholds were not retrieved. The Oct 2026 change to free utility messages inside the CSW is unconfirmed (sources conflict).
- Full wording of Meta's opt-in policy and official Gupshup and Meta-direct pricing were not retrieved.
- There is no reliable figure for what Indian schools typically pay per month for WhatsApp, and no India-specific study on WhatsApp group problems or DPDP guidance for schools.

## 2. SMS in India: DLT, templates, cost, school use

### Takeaway
SMS still works and is cheap, at about Rs 0.10-0.25 per transactional SMS. It requires TRAI DLT registration of the entity (about Rs 5,900 one-time), each sender header and each exact template. Since 6 May 2025 networks add a -P, -S, -T or -G suffix to the header. SMS is the fallback channel for parents without WhatsApp, but vendors claim lower engagement than WhatsApp. That claim is unsourced.

### Cited Findings
- DLT has three layers: entity registration, header (sender ID) registration and content template registration. Real-time network scrubbing blocks any message that does not exactly match an approved template. Registering on one operator's DLT portal covers all networks, and only Indian entities can register — [Telerivet](https://www.telerivet.com/blog/india-sms-compliance-trai-dlt-registration-and-tcccpr-guide); [EnableX guide](https://www.enablex.io/insights/a-step-by-step-guide-to-dlt-registration/)
- From 6 May 2025 TRAI requires operators to append suffixes to A2P headers: P (promotional), S (service), T (transactional), G (government). Operators add them during scrubbing — [EnableX / vendor guides](https://www.enablex.io/insights/a-step-by-step-guide-to-dlt-registration/); [Zoho Campaigns help](https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/campaigns/faqs/sms-campaigns/dlt-registration/articles/sms-campaigns-27-3-2026). One vendor says the Feb 2025 amendments removed the "Service Explicit" category, but other guides still list it, so this is unverified.
- Promotional SMS is barred between 21:00 and 10:00 — [Telerivet](https://www.telerivet.com/blog/india-sms-compliance-trai-dlt-registration-and-tcccpr-guide)
- Fees: Principal Entity registration about Rs 5,900 (Rs 5,000 + GST) one-time, each header about Rs 5,900, templates free — [vendor guides via search: EnableX](https://www.enablex.io/insights/a-step-by-step-guide-to-dlt-registration/)
- Per-SMS vendor estimates: transactional Rs 0.10-0.18 and promotional Rs 0.08-0.12 by one estimate; transactional Rs 0.15-0.25 by another. OTPs cost about Rs 0.10-0.20 at Message Central, about Rs 0.15 at MSG91 and about Rs 0.17 at Gupshup. Fast2SMS charges Rs 0.11 at a Rs 6 lakh top-up, reverting to Rs 0.25 — [Message Central](https://www.messagecentral.com/en-in/blog/a2p-sms-pricing-india), [Decipher](https://decipherconsultancy.in/whatsapp-vs-sms-transactional.html), [Fast2SMS pricing via getpulsesignal](https://getpulsesignal.com/pricing/fast2sms)
- A vendor claims SMS fee reminders get a 14% response against 78% on WhatsApp, and that paper, app and SMS together reach 22-28% of parents. These figures are unsourced vendor claims — [RichAutomate](https://richautomate.in/blog/whatsapp-k12-schools-india-2026)

### Inferences
- SMS per-message cost is about the same as WhatsApp utility, so the choice turns on reach and engagement, not price. SMS needs no app or opt-in flow beyond DLT and reaches feature phones.
- If SchoolOS sends SMS under its own entity for all schools, each school's header and templates still have to be registered. One DLT registration per school (school as Principal Entity) is cleaner but adds onboarding friction.

### Gaps
- No independent data on how many Indian or AP schools still use SMS, and no official TRAI or operator rate card.

## 3. Parent apps: adoption, preferences, Telugu

### Takeaway
No credible independent survey compares Indian parents' use of school apps with WhatsApp, or breaks results out by tier-2/3 towns. The available evidence is vendor claims on both sides. Treat "parents prefer WhatsApp in tier-2/3" as a plausible hypothesis, not an established fact. SchoolOS's English-first stance (ADR-0036) conflicts with any claim that Telugu messaging would raise parent reach, and that claim is itself unsupported.

### Cited Findings
- The All India Parent Survey 2024 (K-12 Techno Services, more than 3,000 parents) found 87% support technology integration in schools. It does not cover messaging channels — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/87-percent-parents-support-tech-integration-schools-28-restrict-usage-educational-purposes-study)
- A vendor (WhatsApp seller) claims app push notifications "nobody opens" and lists app-only homework and attendance, which reaches only one parent, as a failure mode — [RichAutomate](https://richautomate.in/blog/whatsapp-k12-schools-india-2026)
- A vendor (app seller) claims 94% app adoption at a Jaipur school, with WhatsApp as a fallback. This is unverified marketing — [case study page](https://gleeful-whisper-cloud.lovable.app/case-studies/modern-vidyalaya-parent-app)
- CBGA cites Lokniti-CSDS (2019): about 31% of Indians use WhatsApp daily. The figure is old and general — [CBGA](https://www.cbgaindia.org/blog/whatsapp-school-opportunities-challenges/)
- WhatsApp BSPs targeting schools offer per-parent language preference that includes Telugu. One claims, without support, that multilingual circulars "double" reach — [RichAutomate](https://richautomate.in/blog/whatsapp-k12-schools-india-2026)

### Inferences
- Because app-install evidence is weak and vendor-biased, a lean design would put WhatsApp utility templates (plus SMS fallback) on the receiving end, with a mobile-web page (fee payment, receipt, circular) opened from the message link instead of a native app.
- Telugu templates could be added later behind `SOS_TELUGU_ENABLED`. Meta templates are approved per language, so each Telugu variant would need its own approval.

### Gaps
- No Play Store install or rating data for school parent apps was collected. No AP or Telugu-specific parent-communication study was found.

## 4. Fee collection: AP practice, gateways, MDR, UPI AutoPay, RBI PA rules, GST

### Takeaway
Bank-account UPI still carries zero MDR by law as of Aug 2026. A Sept 2026 article describes a 0.40% MDR above Rs 2,000, capped at Rs 300, but no notification was found, so this is unconfirmed. Gateways charge a "platform fee" of about 2% + 18% GST on standard plans even on UPI, which schools often pass on as a convenience fee. Under the RBI PA Master Direction of 15 Sept 2025, anyone who holds and settles merchants' funds must be an RBI-authorised PA (net worth Rs 15 crore at application, Rs 25 crore by year 3). SchoolOS should therefore not touch funds. It should integrate a licensed PA (Razorpay, Cashfree, Easebuzz, PayU) with each school as the merchant of record, onboarded through the PA's partner/ISV programme. School fees up to higher secondary are GST-exempt. SaaS sold to schools is taxable (18% expected; not confirmed from a primary source).

### Cited Findings
- RBI issued the Regulation of Payment Aggregators Directions on 15 Sept 2025 (RBI/DPSS/2025-26/141). They consolidate and replace the 2020/2021 PA-PG guidelines and the 2023 cross-border directions, and create three categories: PA-Online, PA-Physical and PA-Cross Border — [IndiaCorpLaw](https://indiacorplaw.in/2025/10/09/decoding-rbis-overhaul-of-the-payment-aggregator-directions/); [Saraf & Partners](https://sarafpartners.com/?p=37896); [RBI notification](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12896)
- A PA "facilitates aggregation of payments made by customers to the merchants ... and subsequently settles the collected funds to such merchants". A PA may aggregate funds "only for the merchant with whom it has a contractual relationship". PAs are prohibited from running marketplaces. "Payment gateways remain outside the licensing perimeter" (technology-only) — [IndiaCorpLaw](https://indiacorplaw.in/2025/10/09/decoding-rbis-overhaul-of-the-payment-aggregator-directions/); [Outlook Money](https://outlookmoney.com/banking/payment-aggregator-guidelines-rbi-issues-new-directions-for-payment-aggregators)
- Non-bank PAs need prior RBI authorisation. Net worth must be Rs 15 crore at application and Rs 25 crore by the third financial year. Funds sit in escrow and must be settled within prescribed timelines. Merchant due diligence allows simplified KYC for small merchants (contact-point verification, PAN, one valid document, CKYCR as the primary source) — [IndiaCorpLaw](https://indiacorplaw.in/2025/10/09/decoding-rbis-overhaul-of-the-payment-aggregator-directions/)
- Razorpay's partner programme: "Technology Partners (ISVs)" embed payments while Razorpay "manages PayFac risk, licensing, and compliance" and the platform onboards sub-merchants. Partners can pre-fill sub-merchant KYC, create payment links and initiate refunds. Commission is "by default ... a percentage of the transaction value", with payout 30 days after invoice and a minimum of 3 sub-merchants for service partners. Route split settlement uses linked accounts — [Razorpay partner docs: commissions](https://razorpay.com/docs/partners/commissions.md), [Razorpay partner FAQs](https://razorpay.com/docs/partners/faqs.md), [partner T&C example](https://razorpay.com/partners/kasicare-tnc/), [Razorpay partners](https://razorpay.com/us/partners/)
- Zero MDR on bank-account UPI and RuPay debit has applied since 2020. As of 5 Aug 2026 the government was "exploring" an MDR on business UPI through an amendment to the PSS Act, reportedly only above Rs 2,000, but nothing had been notified. The Payments Council of India said in March 2025 that UPI costs about Rs 10,000 crore a year against a Rs 1,500 crore incentive — [The Week, Aug 2026](https://www.theweek.in/news/biz-tech/2026/08/05/will-the-government-levy-fees-for-upi-payments-above-indian-rupee2000.amp.html)
- CONFLICT: Outlook Money (16 Sept 2026) describes bank-account UPI as nil MDR up to Rs 2,000 and 0.40% above, capped at Rs 300 from Rs 75,000. It says education fees fall in an "Industry Programme" category. It cites no notification — [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/banking/upi-mdr-varies-by-payment-method-understanding-rates-caps-and-concessions). This may describe a proposal, so treat it as unconfirmed.
- Razorpay separates MDR (zero on bank UPI by RBI rule) from its platform fee. Its standard pricing example is 2% + 18% GST. New merchants get a 0% platform fee for 90 days or Rs 5 lakh GMV, whichever ends first — [Razorpay blog: UPI charges](https://razorpay.com/blog/upi-charges-explained-mdr-vs-platform-fees/), [Razorpay 0% offer 2026](https://razorpay.com/blog/razorpay-0-percent-platform-fee-offer-90-days-new-merchants-2026/)
- Cards: RuPay debit 0% MDR, other debit up to about 0.9%, credit 1.5-2.5%. A Canara Bank schedule lists Education at 0.70% for RuPay credit cards on UPI (others 1.75%) — [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/banking/upi-mdr-varies-by-payment-method-understanding-rates-caps-and-concessions); [Canara Bank](https://www.canarabank.bank.in/documents/d/guest/mdr-on-rupay-credit-card-on-upi-payments)
- Easebuzz positions itself as an RBI-licensed PA specialising in education fees and NGO donations. It claims 25,000+ merchants, "0% MDR on UPI, ~2% on cards" (platform fee separate), e-NACH and UPI AutoPay for instalments, a defaulter dashboard and Tally integration. These claims come from an integrator page — [PrecisionTech](https://precisiontech.in/solutions/payment-gateway-integration/easebuzz/)
- UPI AutoPay: the default per-execution limit without additional authentication is Rs 15,000. NPCI raised it to Rs 1 lakh only for mutual funds, insurance premiums and credit card bills (Dec 2023), and education was not shown in that list — [Cashfree UPI AutoPay](https://www.cashfree.com/en/upi-autopay/); [NPCI circular reference](https://www.npci.org.in/PDF/npci/rupay/2022/Circular-RuPay-AutoPay-AFA-Limit-enhancement-and-compliance.pdf). The UPI per-transaction limit for payments to educational institutions was raised to Rs 5 lakh — [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/tax-cases/rbi-raises-upi-transaction-limit-hospitals-educational-institutions-244062). PhonePe claims higher AutoPay limits for education "as of late 2025", but cites no circular — [PhonePe](https://business.phonepe.com/articles/how-to-accept-subscription-payments-in-india-upi-auto-pay-vs-e-nach-vs-cards)
- Reconciliation pain, from vendor sources: a parent pays online but the accounts team manually updates a separate fee register. Integrated ERP-gateway setups sync payments to student ledgers, and running two gateways for failover and routing is common advice — [EnKash](https://www.enkash.com/resources/blog/erp-integrated-payment-gateway-for-education-platforms-the-complete-guide); [OpenEduCat glossary](https://openeducat.org/de/glossary/fee-management-system-definition/). Gateways commonly named for Indian education are Razorpay, PayU, Cashfree, CCAvenue, ICICI/HDFC, Easebuzz and Paytm — same sources; [Pine Labs education](https://www.pinelabs.com/solutions/education-industry)
- AP fee regulation: the AP School Education Regulatory and Monitoring Commission set fee caps for 2021-22 to 2023-24 by location. Gram panchayat schools were capped at Rs 10,000 (primary) and Rs 12,000 (higher classes), municipalities at Rs 11,000 and Rs 15,000, municipal corporations at Rs 12,000 and Rs 18,000. Transport was capped at Rs 1.60/km — [The News Minute](https://www.thenewsminute.com/amp/story/andhra-pradesh/education-should-not-become-business-andhra-govt-fee-structure-pvt-schools-154440). Schools challenged the caps — [The News Minute](https://www.thenewsminute.com/article/andhra-schools-cry-foul-over-govt-s-move-fix-ceiling-tuition-fee-154508). The commission has reported schools charging above the norms — [The News Minute](https://www.thenewsminute.com/amp/story/andhra-pradesh/pvt-schools-ap-flouting-govt-norms-charging-excessive-fees-regulatory-commission-118395). A High Court judgment held that location alone cannot be the basis for grouping institutions — [SupremeToday](https://supremetoday.ai/doc/judgement/00200054504). No 2025-26 school fee order was found.
- GST: education up to higher secondary is exempt, and so are listed auxiliary services supplied to such institutions (SAC 9992). Other education-related services are taxed at 18%, and schools cannot claim ITC for exempt services — [Masters India](https://www.mastersindia.co/blog/gst-on-educational-services-india/); [Bajaj Finserv](https://bajajfinserv.in/gst-on-educational-services); [Axis Bank](https://www.axis.bank.in/blogs/taxation/gst-on-education-services). There is conflicting information on school transport (5% vs 18%) — same sources.

### Inferences
- Under the RBI definition, SchoolOS must never receive parent money into its own account and then pay schools. Doing so would make it a PA needing RBI authorisation and Rs 15-25 crore net worth. The compliant pattern is for the school to be the PA's merchant (its own KYC and settlement to its own bank account), with SchoolOS creating payment links and orders and reading webhooks for reconciliation. Any platform commission comes from the PA's partner programme. This also keeps money out of the `platform` control plane.
- Because GST exemption for "auxiliary services" covers only listed items supplied to schools, SchoolOS subscriptions are likely to attract 18% GST. Schools cannot claim ITC, so the GST is a real cost to them. Confirm with a tax adviser.
- Cash at the counter remains a likely need, but no sourced data confirms it for AP. If so, receipts must cover cash, UPI QR (static QR from the school's bank, reconciled by UTR) and gateway links equally. The AP fee caps also make a per-school, per-class fee structure plus a regulatory-cap check a plausible feature.
- A 2% platform fee is material on a Rs 15,000-30,000 annual fee. Many tier-2 schools are therefore likely to prefer a direct bank UPI QR, which is free but leaves manual reconciliation, over gateway links. Supporting both, with UTR-based matching, would address the reconciliation pain.

### Gaps
- No sourced data on the share of AP private-school fees paid in cash, UPI QR, gateway or bank challan, or on typical instalment, concession and late-fee practice. These would need primary interviews.
- Official Razorpay, Cashfree, PayU and Easebuzz education pricing pages and settlement cycles (T+1/T+2) were not retrieved.
- No CBIC primary text for GST on SaaS sold to schools. Whether the Sept 2026 UPI MDR has been notified is unconfirmed.
- No confirmed NPCI circular placing education in a higher UPI AutoPay limit.

## 5. Fee financing / EMI players and ERP partnerships

### Takeaway
GrayQuest (6,500+ institutions, Rs 80 crore Series B in Jan 2025) and Jodo offer parents "no-cost" monthly instalments, and the school receives the full fee upfront. Propelld does fee EMIs mostly in higher education. Varthana lends to schools, not parents. No confirmed GrayQuest or Jodo partnership with a K-12 ERP was found. Collexo, a higher-education fee portal, integrates both Propelld and GrayQuest, which shows that ERP-embedded EMI exists.

### Cited Findings
- GrayQuest raised about Rs 80 crore ($9.3M) in Series B in Jan 2025, led by IIFL Fintech Fund, Claypond Capital and Pravega. It claims 6,500+ institutions (K-12 and universities) and offers monthly payment of annual fees "with no extra costs to students and parents" — [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/news/grayquest-raises-93-mn-from-iifl-fintech-fund-claypond-and-pravega-8605812); [CXO Today](https://cxotoday.com/media-coverage/grayquest-raises-rs-80-crore-9-3m-in-its-series-b-equity-funding-round/)
- Jodo (Bengaluru): with "Jodo Cred", parents pay fees monthly while "institutions receive the fee upfront without any risk of delay or non-payment". "Jodo Flex" is recurring auto-pay. Jodo raised $15M (Series A) — [Jodo about](https://jodo.in/about-us); [Inc42](https://inc42.com/?p=350173). A Jodo blog (Mar 2025) claims no-cost EMI loans carry an implied interest of about 25% a year. This is a company claim — [Jodo blog](https://www.jodo.in/blog/product/is-flex-better-than-cred/)
- Varthana lends to affordable private schools (infrastructure, resources). It raised Rs 159 crore in debt in July 2025 — [Entrepreneur India](https://india.entrepreneur.com/?p=83161)
- Propelld provides 0% EMIs for BITS Pilani applicants, and a university page lists 6/10/18/24-month EMIs for "Annual School Fee" (undated). The Collexo fee portal integrates Propelld and GrayQuest EMIs — [BITS WILP](https://bits-pilani-wilp.ac.in/emi-option-business-analytics.php); [Chanakya University](https://chanakyauniversity.edu.in/admission/financial-aid/); [Collexo: Propelld](https://help.collexo.com/portal/en/kb/articles/introducing-monthly-installments-with-propelld); [Collexo: GrayQuest](https://help.collexo.com/portal/en/kb/articles/introducing-emis-for-applicants-with-grayquest)

### Inferences
- In a "no-cost EMI" the interest is usually borne by the school as a subvention discount, so the school receives less than face value. Jodo's critique implies this. Any SchoolOS integration would be a referral or redirect to the lender's flow, not lending. Credit decisions on parents would also raise invariant 14 (purpose limit) concerns if student data were shared.

### Gaps
- Subvention rates schools pay, and penetration of EMI in tier-2/3 AP schools, were not found. No K-12 ERP-lender partnership terms were found.

## 6. Revenue models: ERP vendors earning from payment volume

### Takeaway
The mechanism exists: PA partner programmes pay ISVs a percentage of transaction value (Razorpay's default), and Route-style splits are possible. However, no public data shows Indian school ERPs earning materially from fee volume. Teachmint's reported revenue comes from software and hardware sales.

### Cited Findings
- Razorpay partner commission is "by default ... a percentage of the transaction value". A partner can alternatively add its own fee (flat or %) on the customer invoice, per the Curlec docs — [Razorpay commissions](https://razorpay.com/docs/partners/commissions.md); [Curlec commissions](https://curlec.com/docs/partners/commissions/)
- Teachmint's FY25 operating revenue was Rs 74 crore (4.3x), from sales of products and related services. Its FY24 income came from selling software to schools and devices (biometrics, interactive flat panels, GPS). There is no mention of fee-collection commission — [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/fintrackr/teachmints-revenue-jumps-43x-in-fy25-10550884); [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/teachmint-cuts-fy24-loss-to-inr-110-cr-revenue-soars-111/)
- Entab does not publish pricing (custom quotes) and markets online fee payment with reminders — [Entab](https://www.entab.in/benefits-of-school-fee-management-software.html)

### Inferences
- SchoolOS could earn a PA partner commission, which needs disclosure to schools and audit, with money flows staying inside the PA. A convenience fee charged to parents would be visible and is often resented, which matters given AP fee-regulation sensitivity. Building revenue on payment volume would conflict with any future UPI MDR change and adds dependency on the PA's terms.

### Gaps
- No public commission rates (bps) for ISV partners at Razorpay, Cashfree or PayU. No Inc42 or Entrackr reporting on school-ERP fee-volume revenue.
