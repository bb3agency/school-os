# Audit 2026-11-09: money (fees, online payments, Tally) (YF)

Base `fad0b02c`. Round 2, a deep pass per module. Scope: `app/fees` (heads, plans and plan
versions, accounts, the append-only ledger, gap-free receipts and refund vouchers, concessions,
adjustments, cancellations and refunds with maker-checker, late fees, reports and the day
close, UPI and bank statements), online payments (`online.py`, the Razorpay provider, the
webhook verifier and inbox, refunds, reconciliation) and `app/tally` (the read-only connector,
dues and the edge agent API), with their tasks and the migrations that made their tables
(`0036_tally`, `0058_fees_core`, `0059_payments_online`, `0060_fees_upi`, `0077_tally_bills`).
The audits of 2026-10-04 to 2026-10-17, round 1 of 2026-11-08 (BL-04, BL-06, BL-07, CP-01) and
the web round-2 fees report (XF-*) were read first. Their fixed items are not repeated here.

**Method.** Each fixed finding has a test that failed on the code before the fix and passes
after it. Data is synthetic only. Paths are relative to `apps/api/`.

## Checked and not a bug

- **Money.** Amounts are `Decimal` `numeric(14,2)` rupees end to end, by design (FR-FEE-028). Paise appear only in `providers/razorpay.py` `to_paise`/`from_paise`, which refuse floats and fractional paise. Schemas use `max_digits=14, decimal_places=2`. Percent concessions round half up to the paisa. The `in_words` and Indian grouping helpers are exact.
- **Ledger.** Entry signs and sources are pinned by `ledger_entries_sign` and `ledger_entries_source`. Allocations, cancellations, refunds, counters, accounts and requests have append-only or frozen triggers. Charges and late fees have unique keys (`ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`). A receipt's allocations and payment entries add up to its amount (deferred trigger).
- **Gap-free numbers.** `allocate_number` is `INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE … RETURNING`, so the counter row stays locked until commit. A rollback after the number was taken (including the inbox savepoint rollback on `_Retry`) releases it. Two counters (`RCT`, `RFD`) are separate rows per financial year. The financial year is computed from the IST receipt or voucher date (`c.fy`, April start). An online capture on 31 March processed on 1 April is numbered in the old year's series, as its receipt date says.
- **Day close.** Receipts (counter and online), cancellations, cash/cheque/bank refunds and online refund vouchers all call `ensure_day_open` (shared advisory day lock) before writing. `close_day` takes the lock exclusively, then computes the totals in a fresh statement. Concessions, adjustments, charges and late fees are not collections and are outside FR-FEE-019.
- **Refund and cancel limits.** A refund is at most the receipt amount less pending, approved, processing or processed refunds, checked under the receipt lock both at request and at approval. Its parts are capped per allocation line. A cancellation is refused once any refund is committed, and a refund once the receipt is cancelled. Only one live cancellation is allowed per receipt (unique index). An online receipt is cancelled only as `duplicate_entry`. Razorpay also caps refunds at the payment amount.
- **Maker-checker.** `_decide_checks` refuses the requester (403 `self_approval_forbidden`). The DB `*_maker_checker` CHECKs back it. Step-up and `If-Match` are checked on every approve and reject. Withdraw is allowed for the maker only. Lock order is request, receipt, account, the same as the counter (account only).
- **Webhooks.** HMAC-SHA256 over the raw body is compared in constant time against the current secret and, for 7 days, the previous one. The route handle resolves before anything is read. The event id is the replay key and the inbox's unique key. `post_payment` never trusts the webhook's figures: it re-fetches the payment and order, matches intent, amount and currency, and checks `receipt_for_payment` under the account and intent locks. Refunds are re-fetched and their amount compared. The minimised payload is stored encrypted.
- **Tasks.** The inbox uses `SKIP LOCKED` with one event per transaction and a savepoint. The late-fee job and the online tasks run one `tenant_session` per school, and one failing school does not stop the others. Receipt PDF rendering is idempotent (a stored document is left alone). `send_refund` uses the refund id as Razorpay's idempotency reference. Logs carry ids and codes only.
- **Online links.** The amount is computed server-side from the dues of the chosen instalments, with `accept_partial: false`. The provider's echo of amount and reference is checked. Overlapping links are closed. Open links are capped per account.
- **UPI statements.** Lines and receipts are locked and the pair is re-checked (amount, UTR, issued, not already confirmed). A receipt is never changed by a confirmation.
- **Tally.** Snapshots serialise on the company's parties. An older `as_of` is refused, and a future one is refused by the schema. Batch ids are unique per device. Parties are capped at 20,000 and bills at 100 per party. Unique constraints on guid and name back the first sync. Dues are scoped per student (BL-07).
